## Project Overview

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. She has recorded her daily sales for two weeks and maintains records of her stock quantities. This project uses Python to analyse her business performance, identify sales trends, compare weekday and weekend performance, identify products that need restocking, and calculate gross profit margins. The goal is to provide practical, data-driven recommendations to help Wanjiku improve her business.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Weekly sales increased by 10.66%.** Week 1 generated KES 22,050, while Week 2 generated KES 24,400.

2. **Weekends performed better than weekdays.** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900 compared with weekday average sales of KES 2,685, a difference of 82.50%.

3. **Two products need restocking.** Tomatoes had 18 kg against a reorder level of 25 kg, while sukuma had 9 kg against a reorder level of 15 kg.
